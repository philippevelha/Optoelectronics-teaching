Appendix B — Formula sheet

All the key relations of Chapter 5 on one page. Each block links to the chapter where the formula is derived and used. Use your browser’s Print → Save as PDF for a paper copy.

NoteSymbols and constants

\(\eta_e\) external QE · \(\eta_i\) internal QE · \(\mathcal R\) responsivity · \(I_{ph}\) photocurrent · \(I_d\) dark current · \(P_o\) optical power · α absorption coefficient · \(W\) depletion (i-layer) width · \(v_d\) drift velocity · \(D\) diffusion coefficient · \(M\) avalanche multiplication · \(k=\alpha_h/\alpha_e\) · \(F\) excess noise factor · \(B\) bandwidth · \(R_L\) load · \(C_t\) total capacitance · FF fill factor

\(h=6.626\times10^{-34}\) J s · \(hc=1.2398\) eV µm · \(k_BT=25.85\) meV at 300 K · \(e=1.602\times10^{-19}\) C · \(4k_BT=1.66\times10^{-20}\) J at 300 K · \(2e=3.2\times10^{-19}\) C

B.1 Junction photodiode (Chapter 1)

illuminated diode \(I=I_o[e^{eV/\eta k_BT}-1]-I_{ph}\)
short circuit \(I_{sc}=-I_{ph}\)
open circuit \(V_{oc}=\frac{\eta k_BT}{e}\ln(1+I_{ph}/I_o)\)
Shockley–Ramo (Chapter 2) \(i_e=ev_e/L\) for \(t<(L-l)/v_e\); \(\int(i_e+i_h)dt=e\)
single-carrier transit \(f_{3dB}=0.443\,v/W\)

B.2 Absorption and responsivity (Chapter 3, Chapter 4)

Beer–Lambert \(I(x)=I_oe^{-\alpha x}\), \(\delta=1/\alpha\)
cut-off \(\lambda_g\,[\mu\text m]=1.24/E_g\,[\text{eV}]\)
QE \(\eta_e=(I_{ph}/e)/(P_o/h\nu)\)
responsivity \(\mathcal R=\eta_e e\lambda/hc=\eta_e\lambda[\mu\text m]/1.24\) A/W
max QE tangent to \(\mathcal R(\lambda)\) through the origin
structure \(I_{ph}=\frac{e\eta_iTP_o}{h\nu}[e^{-\alpha(\ell_p-L_e)}-e^{-\alpha(\ell_p+W+L_h)}]\)
bare surface \(T=1-[(n-1)/(n+1)]^2\)

B.3 pin photodiode (Chapter 5)

field \(E\approx V_r/W\)
capacitance \(C_\text{dep}=\varepsilon_o\varepsilon_rA/W\)
drift time \(t_\text{drift}=W/v_d\), \(v_d\to v_\text{sat}\approx10^5\) m/s (Si)
diffusion \(\ell=\sqrt{2Dt}\), \(t_\text{diff}=\ell^2/2D\)
steady state \(I_{ph}=\frac{e\eta_iTP_o}{h\nu}(1-e^{-\alpha W})\)
optimum width \(f_\text{tr}=f_{RC}\): \(W_\text{opt}\approx\sqrt{0.9\pi R\varepsilon Av_s}\)

B.4 Avalanche photodiode (Chapter 6, Chapter 11)

gain \(M=I_{ph}/I_{pho}\), \(\mathcal R_{APD}=M\mathcal R\)
empirical \(M=1/[1-(V_r/V_{br})^m]\)
Chynoweth \(\alpha=A\exp(-B/E)\)
electrons only \(M=e^{\alpha_ew}\)
both carriers \(M=\dfrac{1-k}{e^{-(1-k)\alpha_ew}-k}\)
APD noise \(i_n^2=2e(I_{do}+I_{pho})M^2FB\)
excess noise \(F\approx M^x\); \(F=kM+(1-k)(2-1/M)\)
optimum gain \(M_\text{opt}^{2+x}=4k_BT/(xeR_LI_{pho})\)

B.5 Schottky, MSM, phototransistor, photoconductor (Chapter 7, Chapter 8)

barrier \(\Phi_B=\Phi_m-\chi\)
dark current \(J=A^*T^2e^{-\Phi_B/k_BT}\)
internal photoemission \(\eta=C_1(h\nu-\Phi_B)^2/h\nu\)
phototransistor \(I_{ph}\approx\beta I_{pho}\), \(f\approx I_{pho}/(2\pi(k_BT/e)C)\)
photoconductivity \(\Delta\sigma=e\eta_iI\lambda\tau(\mu_e+\mu_h)/hcd\)
photoconductive gain \(G=\tau/t_e+\tau/t_h\), \(t=\ell/\mu E\)
gain × bandwidth \(G\cdot\frac{1}{2\pi\tau}=\frac{1}{2\pi t_e}\)

B.6 Circuits and speed (Chapter 9)

load line \(I=-(V+V_r)/R_L\)
TIA \(V_\text{out}=-I_{ph}R_F\), \(f\approx\sqrt{\text{GBW}/2\pi R_FC_d}\)
cut-off \(f_c=1/2\pi R_\text{eq}C_t\approx1/2\pi R_LC_t\)
rise/fall time \(\tau_r=\tau_F=2.2RC\)
bandwidth–rise time \(f_c=0.35/\tau_r\)

B.7 Noise (Chapter 10)

shot noise \(i_n=[2e(I_d+I_{ph})B]^{1/2}\)
thermal noise \(i_\text{th}=[4k_BTB/R_L]^{1/2}\)
SNR \(\dfrac{I_{ph}^2}{2e(I_d+I_{ph})B+4k_BTB/R_L}\)
NEP \(P_1/\sqrt B=[2e(I_d+I_{ph})]^{1/2}/\mathcal R\)
detectivity \(D^*=\sqrt A/\text{NEP}\) (Jones)
ideal detector \(P_1=2hcB/\eta\lambda\)

B.8 Receivers (Chapter 14)

Q factor \(Q=(I_1-I_0)/(\sigma_1+\sigma_0)\)
BER \(\frac12\operatorname{erfc}(Q/\sqrt2)\); \(10^{-9}\): \(Q=6\)
thermal-limited pin \(\bar P=Q\sigma_T/\mathcal R\)
shot-limited pin \(\bar P=Q^2eB_e/\mathcal R\)
quantum limit \(\frac12e^{-N_1}\): 10 photons/bit at \(10^{-9}\)

B.9 Image sensors (Chapter 12)

signal \(S=\eta\Phi_{ph}A_\text{pix}t_\text{exp}\le N_\text{FW}\)
pixel SNR \(S/\sqrt{S+D+\sigma_r^2}\)
dynamic range \(N_\text{FW}/\sigma_r\)
charge transfer fraction kept \(\text{CTE}^N\)

B.10 Solar cells (Chapter 13, Chapter 15)

I–V \(I=-I_{ph}+I_o[e^{e(V-IR_s)/\eta k_BT}-1]+(V-IR_s)/R_p\)
photocurrent \(I_{ph}=KI_\text{light}\)
open circuit \(V_{oc}=\frac{\eta k_BT}{e}\ln(I_{ph}/I_o)\)
fill factor \(\text{FF}=I_mV_m/I_{sc}V_{oc}\)
efficiency \(\text{FF}I_{sc}V_{oc}/(I_\text{light}A)\)
SQ limit ≈ 30.5 % at 1.3 eV (black body), 33.7 % (AM1.5)

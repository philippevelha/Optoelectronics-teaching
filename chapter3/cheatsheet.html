NoteSymbols and constants

\(E_c,E_v\) band edges · \(E_g=E_c-E_v\) · \(E_F\) Fermi level (\(E_{Fi}\) intrinsic, \(E_{Fn},E_{Fp}\) n/p side or quasi-Fermi levels) · \(m_e^*,m_h^*\) effective masses · \(N_d,N_a\) donor/acceptor concentrations · \(n,p\) electron/hole concentrations · \(\mu\) drift mobility · \(D\) diffusion coefficient · \(\tau\) lifetime · \(L\) diffusion length · \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r\) · \(V\) applied voltage (> 0 forward) · \(V_\text{th}=V_T=k_BT/e\)

\(k_BT=25.85\) meV at 300 K · \(hc=1.2398\) eV µm · \(e=1.602\times10^{-19}\) C · \(\varepsilon_0=8.854\times10^{-12}\) F/m · \(n_i\) (300 K): Si \(1\times10^{10}\), GaAs \(2.1\times10^6\), Ge \(2.3\times10^{13}\) cm⁻³

B.1 Bands and statistics (Chapter 1, Chapter 2)

density of states \(g(E)=\dfrac{8\sqrt2\,\pi m^{*3/2}}{h^3}(E-E_c)^{1/2}\)
Fermi–Dirac \(f(E)=\dfrac{1}{1+e^{(E-E_F)/k_BT}}\)
Fermi energy of a metal \(E_{F0}=\dfrac{h^2}{8m_e}\left(\dfrac{3n}{\pi}\right)^{2/3}\)
electrons in CB \(n=N_ce^{-(E_c-E_F)/k_BT}\)
holes in VB \(p=N_ve^{-(E_F-E_v)/k_BT}\)
effective DOS \(N_{c,v}=2\left(\dfrac{2\pi m^*_{e,h}k_BT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}\)
mass action law \(np=n_i^2=N_cN_ve^{-E_g/k_BT}\)
intrinsic Fermi level \(E_{Fi}=E_v+\tfrac12E_g-\tfrac34k_BT\ln(m_e^*/m_h^*)\)
mean electron KE in CB \(\tfrac32k_BT\)
validity (non-degenerate) \(E_F\) more than \(3k_BT\) from the band edges
crystal momentum \(\psi_k=U_k(x)e^{jkx}\), \(F=d(\hbar k)/dt\)

B.2 Doping and transport (Chapter 3)

n-type (\(N_d\gg n_i\)) \(n=N_d\), \(p=n_i^2/N_d\)
p-type (\(N_a\gg n_i\)) \(p=N_a\), \(n=n_i^2/N_a\)
compensation (\(N_d>N_a\)) \(n=N_d-N_a\), \(p=n_i^2/(N_d-N_a)\)
general neutrality \(n=\tfrac{N_d-N_a}{2}+\sqrt{\left(\tfrac{N_d-N_a}{2}\right)^2+n_i^2}\)
Fermi level shift \(E_{Fn}-E_{Fi}=k_BT\ln(n/n_i)\), \(E_{Fp}-E_{Fi}=-k_BT\ln(p/n_i)\)
drift velocity \(v_d=\mu E\)
conductivity \(\sigma=en\mu_e+ep\mu_h\)
Einstein relation \(D=\mu k_BT/e\)
applied voltage \(\Delta E_F=eV\)

B.3 pn junction at equilibrium (Chapter 4)

Poisson \(d^2V/dx^2=-\rho_\text{net}/\varepsilon\)
charge balance \(N_aW_p=N_dW_n\)
built-in voltage \(V_o=\dfrac{k_BT}{e}\ln\dfrac{N_aN_d}{n_i^2}\)
depletion width \(W=\left[\dfrac{2\varepsilon(N_a+N_d)(V_o-V)}{eN_aN_d}\right]^{1/2}\)
peak field \(E_o=-eN_dW_n/\varepsilon=-2(V_o-V)/W\)
law of the junction \(p_{no}/p_{po}=n_{po}/n_{no}=e^{-eV_o/k_BT}\)
from work functions \(eV_o=\Phi_p-\Phi_n=E_{Fn}-E_{Fp}\) (before contact)
Debye length \(L_D=\sqrt{\varepsilon k_BT/e^2N}\)

B.4 Diode currents (Chapter 5)

injection \(p_n(0)=p_{no}e^{eV/k_BT}\), \(n_p(0)=n_{po}e^{eV/k_BT}\)
excess profile (long) \(\Delta p_n(x')=\Delta p_n(0)e^{-x'/L_h}\), \(L_h=\sqrt{D_h\tau_h}\)
Shockley equation \(J=J_{so}\left[e^{eV/k_BT}-1\right]\)
saturation current \(J_{so}=\left(\dfrac{eD_h}{L_hN_d}+\dfrac{eD_e}{L_eN_a}\right)n_i^2\)
short diode \(L_h\to\ell_n\), \(L_e\to\ell_p\); transit time \(\tau_t=\ell_n^2/2D_h\)
SCL recombination \(J_\text{rec}=J_{ro}\left[e^{eV/2k_BT}-1\right]\), \(J_{ro}=\dfrac{en_i}{2}\left(\dfrac{W_p}{\tau_e}+\dfrac{W_n}{\tau_h}\right)\)
general diode equation \(J=J_o\left[e^{eV/\eta k_BT}-1\right]\), \(\eta=1\) (diffusion) to 2 (recombination)
reverse current \(J_\text{rev}=J_{so}+en_iW/\tau_g\)
direct-gap lifetime \(\tau=1/(BN)\) (weak injection)

B.5 Small-signal model (Chapter 6)

depletion capacitance \(C_\text{dep}=\dfrac{\varepsilon A}{W}=\dfrac{A}{(V_o-V)^{1/2}}\left[\dfrac{e\varepsilon N_aN_d}{2(N_a+N_d)}\right]^{1/2}\)
p⁺n junction \(C_\text{dep}=A\left[\dfrac{e\varepsilon N_d}{2(V_o-V)}\right]^{1/2}\)
Mott–Schottky \(N_d=-\dfrac{2}{e\varepsilon A^2}\left[\dfrac{d(1/C^2)}{dV}\right]^{-1}\)
dynamic resistance \(r_d=\eta V_\text{th}/I\)
diffusion capacitance long (ac): \(\dfrac{\tau_hI}{2V_\text{th}}=\dfrac{\tau_h}{2r_d}\); short: \(\dfrac{\tau_tI}{V_\text{th}}\)

B.6 LED emission (Chapter 7, Chapter 8)

photon energy \(h\nu\approx E_g\), \(\lambda[\mu\text{m}]=1.24/E_g[\text{eV}]\)
spectrum \(I(h\nu)\propto(h\nu-E_g)^{1/2}e^{-(h\nu-E_g)/k_BT}\)
peak \(h\nu_\text{peak}=E_g+\tfrac12k_BT\)
width \(\Delta(h\nu)\approx1.8k_BT\) (ideal) to \(3k_BT\)
linewidth \(\Delta\lambda\approx\lambda_o^2\,\dfrac{3k_BT}{hc}\) (107 nm at 1310 nm)
Varshni \(E_g(T)=E_{go}-\dfrac{AT^2}{B+T}\) (GaAs: ≈ 0.24 nm/K)
degenerate junction \(\Delta(h\nu)\approx\Delta E_{Fn}+1.5k_BT\)
Vegard / bowing \(a=xa_1+(1-x)a_2\), \(E_g=xE_1+(1-x)E_2-bx(1-x)\)
heterojunction leakage \(\propto e^{-\Delta E_c/k_BT}\)

B.7 Quantum wells (Chapter 9)

infinite well \(\Delta E_n=\dfrac{h^2n^2}{8m^*d^2}\)
in-plane motion \(E=E_c+\Delta E_n+\dfrac{\hbar^2(k_y^2+k_z^2)}{2m^*}\)
2D DOS (per sub-band) \(g_{2D}=\dfrac{m^*}{\pi\hbar^2}\) per unit area
finite well, even \(\dfrac{k}{m_w}\tan\dfrac{kd}{2}=\dfrac{\kappa}{m_b}\)
finite well, odd \(-\dfrac{k}{m_w}\cot\dfrac{kd}{2}=\dfrac{\kappa}{m_b}\)
emission \(h\nu=E_{g1}+E_1+E_1'\)

B.8 Efficiency and photometry (Chapter 10)

escape cone \(\sin\theta_c=n_a/n_s\)
extraction (one face) \(\eta\approx\tfrac12(1-\cos\theta_c)\dfrac{4n_sn_a}{(n_s+n_a)^2}\)
internal QE \(\eta_\text{IQE}=\dfrac{\tau_r^{-1}}{\tau_r^{-1}+\tau_{nr}^{-1}}\)
output power \(P_o=\eta_\text{EE}\eta_\text{IQE}h\nu\,(I/e)\)
external QE \(\eta_\text{EQE}=\dfrac{P_o/h\nu}{I/e}\)
power conversion \(\eta_\text{PCE}=\dfrac{P_o}{IV}\approx\eta_\text{EQE}\dfrac{E_g}{eV}\)
luminous flux \(\Phi_v=683\ \text{lm/W}\times P_oV(\lambda)\)
luminous efficacy \(\eta_\text{LE}=\Phi_v/(IV)\)

B.9 Drive and speed (Chapter 11)

resistor drive \(I_F=(V-V_F)/R\)
net lifetime \(1/\tau=1/\tau_r+1/\tau_{nr}\)
modulation response \(P_o(\omega)/P_o(0)=1/\sqrt{1+(\omega\tau)^2}\)
electrical bandwidth \(f_c=1/(2\pi\tau)\)
optical bandwidth \(f_\text{op}=\sqrt3/(2\pi\tau)\)
rise / fall time \(\tau_R=\tau_F=2.2\tau\)
fiber coupling (Lambertian) \(\eta_c\approx\text{NA}^2\times\min[1,(a/r_s)^2]\)

Appendix B — Formula sheet

All the key relations of Chapter 4 on one page. Each block links to the chapter where the formula is derived and used. Use your browser’s Print → Save as PDF for a paper copy.

NoteSymbols and constants

\(N_1,N_2\) lower/upper level populations · \(N_0\) total ion density · \(\tau_{sp}\) spontaneous lifetime · \(\rho(\nu)\) energy density per unit frequency · \(\sigma_{ab},\sigma_{em}\) cross sections · \(g\) gain coefficient · \(\alpha_s\) internal loss · \(\alpha_t\) total loss · \(R_1,R_2\) mirror reflectances · \(L\) cavity length · \(n\) (group) index · \(\Gamma\) confinement factor · \(\tau_{ph}\) photon lifetime · \(n_{th}\), \(I_{th}\) threshold carrier density and current · \(\tau_r\) radiative lifetime · \(\tau\) carrier lifetime

\(h=6.626\times10^{-34}\) J s · \(hc=1.2398\) eV µm · \(k_BT=25.85\) meV at 300 K · \(e=1.602\times10^{-19}\) C · 1 cm⁻¹ = 100 m⁻¹

B.1 Radiation and Einstein coefficients (Chapter 1, Chapter 2)

mode density (2 polarisations) \(8\pi n^3\nu^2/c^3\)
Planck \(\rho(\nu)=\dfrac{8\pi h\nu^3}{c^3}\dfrac{1}{e^{h\nu/k_BT}-1}\)
rates \(R_{12}=B_{12}N_1\rho\), \(R_{21}=A_{21}N_2+B_{21}N_2\rho\)
Einstein relations \(B_{12}=B_{21}\), \(A_{21}/B_{21}=8\pi h\nu^3n^3/c^3\)
stim./absorption \(N_2/N_1\) → population inversion
stim./spont. \(c^3\rho/8\pi h\nu^3\) = photons per mode → cavity
spontaneous lifetime \(\tau_{sp}=1/A_{21}\)
two-level, pumped \(N_2/N_0=W\tau/(1+2W\tau)<\tfrac12\)

B.2 Pumping (Chapter 3)

3-level inversion \((N_2-N_1)/N_0=(W_p\tau-1)/(W_p\tau+1)\)
4-level inversion \(\approx W_p\tau_{21}\)
min. pump, 3-level \(P_{p,\min}/V=(N_0/2)h\nu_{13}/\tau_{sp}\)
quantum efficiency \(\lambda_p/\lambda_\text{laser}\)

B.3 Gain and the EDFA (Chapter 4)

absorption \(\alpha=\sigma_{ab}N_1\)
gain coefficient \(g=\sigma_{em}N_2-\sigma_{ab}N_1=[\sigma_{em}x-\sigma_{ab}(1-x)]N_0\)
gain \(G=e^{gL}\), \(G_\text{dB}=4.343\,gL\)
power conversion \(\eta_\text{PCE}=(P_{s,o}-P_{s,i})/P_p\le\lambda_p/\lambda_s\)
gain vs PCE \(G=1+\eta_\text{PCE}P_p/P_{s,i}\)
max. input \(P_{s,i}<(\lambda_p/\lambda_s)P_p/(G-1)\)
pump length \(\Gamma P_p\approx AN_0h\nu_pL_p/\tau_{sp}\)
inversion factor \(n_{sp}=\sigma_{em}N_2/(\sigma_{em}N_2-\sigma_{ab}N_1)\)
noise figure \(\text{NF}\approx2n_{sp}(G-1)/G+1/G\ge3\) dB
ASE power \(P_\text{ASE}=2n_{sp}(G-1)h\nu B_o\)

B.4 Cavity, threshold, output (Chapter 5, Chapter 6)

longitudinal modes \(m\lambda_m/2n=L\), \(\nu_m=mc/2nL\)
mode spacing \(\Delta\nu_m=c/2nL\), \(\Delta\lambda_m\approx\lambda^2/2nL\)
Doppler FWHM \(\Delta\nu=2\nu_0\sqrt{2k_BT\ln2/Mc^2}\)
gain vs inversion \(g(\nu_0)\approx(N_2-N_1)B_{21}nh\nu_0/c\Delta\nu\)
threshold gain \(g_{th}=\alpha_t=\alpha_s+\frac{1}{2L}\ln\frac{1}{R_1R_2}\)
threshold inversion \((N_2-N_1)_{th}=g_{th}8\pi n^2\nu_0^2\tau_{sp}\Delta\nu/c^2\)
photon lifetime \(\tau_{ph}=\frac{2nL}{c(1-e^{-2\alpha_tL})}\approx\frac{n}{c\alpha_t}\)
output power \(P_o=\frac12A(1-R_1)h\nu N_\text{ph}c/n\)
finesse \(F=\pi\sqrt R/(1-R)\), mode width \(\Delta\nu_m/F\)

B.5 Line shapes and pulses (Chapter 7, Chapter 8)

Lorentzian \(g_L=\frac{\gamma/\pi}{(\nu-\nu_0)^2+\gamma^2}\), FWHM \(2\gamma\)
lifetime width \(\Delta\nu=1/2\pi\tau\)
Gaussian \(g_G=\frac{1}{\sqrt\pi\Gamma}e^{-(\nu-\nu_0)^2/\Gamma^2}\), FWHM \(2\Gamma\sqrt{\ln2}\)
Q-switch extraction \(r\eta=-\ln(1-\eta)\), \(r=N_i/N_{th}\)
Q-switch peak \(\phi_\text{max}=N_{th}(r-1-\ln r)\)
mode-locked train period \(2L/c\), width \(\approx1/\Delta\nu\), peak \(\approx N\times\) avg.
TEM\(_{mn}\) \(H_m(\sqrt2x/w)H_n(\sqrt2y/w)e^{-(x^2+y^2)/w^2}\)

B.6 Semiconductor gain (Chapter 9, Chapter 13)

inversion condition \(E_g<h\nu<E_{Fn}-E_{Fp}=eV\)
gain spectrum \(g=\alpha_0(h\nu)[f_c(E_2)-f_v(E_1)]\)
near threshold \(g\approx a(n-n_{tr})\) or \(g_0\ln(n/n_{tr})\)
facet reflectance \(R=[(n-1)/(n+1)]^2\approx0.32\)
QW levels \(\varepsilon_n=h^2n^2/8m^*d^2\)
QW emission \(h\nu=E_g+\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon'_1\)

B.7 Laser diode (Chapter 12, Chapter 11, Chapter 10)

rate equations \(\frac{I}{eLWd}=\frac{n}{\tau_r}+CnN_\text{ph}\), \(\frac{N_\text{ph}}{\tau_{ph}}=CnN_\text{ph}\)
threshold \(n_{th}=1/C\tau_{ph}\), \(I_{th}=eLWd\,n_{th}/\tau_r\), \(\tau_r=1/Bn_{th}\)
threshold gain \(\Gamma g_{th}=\alpha_t\)
photons \(N_\text{ph}=\tau_{ph}(I-I_{th})/eLWd\)
output \(P_o=\frac{hc^2\tau_{ph}(1-R)}{2en\lambda L}(I-I_{th})\)
efficiencies \(\eta_\text{slope}=\Delta P_o/\Delta I\), \(\eta_\text{EQE}=\frac{P_o/h\nu}{I/e}\), \(\eta_\text{PCE}=\frac{P_o}{IV}\)
EDQE \(\eta_\text{EDQE}=\eta_\text{EE}\eta_\text{IDQE}\), \(\eta_\text{EE}=\frac{(1/2L)\ln(1/R_1)}{\alpha_t}\)
temperature \(I_{th}=Ae^{T/T_0}\), \(d\lambda_m/dT=(\lambda/n)dn/dT\)

B.8 Single-frequency lasers and amplifiers (Chapter 14, Chapter 15)

Bragg wavelength \(\lambda_B=2n\Lambda/q\)
DFB modes (Kasap) \(\lambda_m=\lambda_B\pm\frac{\lambda_B^2}{2nL}(m+1)\)
grating reflectance \(R=\tanh^2\kappa L\), \(\kappa=\pi\Delta n/\lambda\)
quarter-wave stack \(R=\left(\frac{1-b}{1+b}\right)^2\), \(b=\frac{n_\text{in}}{n_\text{sub}}\left(\frac{n_L}{n_H}\right)^{2N}\)
FP amplifier ripple \(\left[\frac{1+\sqrt{R_1R_2}G_s}{1-\sqrt{R_1R_2}G_s}\right]^2\)
saturation \(G=G_0\exp[-(G-1)P_\text{in}/P_\text{sat}]\)

B.9 Dynamics, linewidth, resonators (Chapter 16, Chapter 19, Chapter 18)

relaxation frequency \(f_r=\frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{\tau\tau_{ph}}}\left(\frac{I_1}{I_{th}}-1\right)^{1/2}\)
turn-on delay \(t_d=\tau\ln\frac{I_2-I_1}{I_2-I_{th}}\)
linewidth \(\Delta\nu=\frac{\pi h\nu\Delta\nu_c^2n_{sp}}{P}(1+\alpha_H^2)\), \(\Delta\nu_c=\frac{1}{2\pi\tau_{ph}}\)
chirp \(\Delta\nu=\frac{\alpha_H}{4\pi}\frac{d\ln P}{dt}\)
stability \(0\le g_1g_2\le1\), \(g_i=1-L/R_i\)
TEM frequencies \(\nu_{qmn}=\frac{c}{2nL}\left[q+(m+n+1)\frac{\arccos\sqrt{g_1g_2}}{\pi}\right]\)
hologram \(U_rI=U_r(\lvert U_r\rvert^2+\lvert U\rvert^2)+\lvert U_r\rvert^2U+U_r^2U^*\)
